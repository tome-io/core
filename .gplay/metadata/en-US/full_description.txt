Tomeio brings book discovery, downloads, reading lists, and your local library together in one place.

Explore curated catalogs, search across your enabled book sources, compare available formats, and save books to your device. Tomeio's extension-based design lets each source provide its own catalogs, metadata, and download options through a consistent interface.

Features:

• Discover books through curated and trending catalogs
• Search across enabled book providers
• Find EPUB, PDF, MOBI, and other available formats
• Organize downloads in a local library
• Track reading progress across supported readers
• Add or manage book-source extensions
• Choose where downloaded books are stored

Availability and download formats depend on the selected source and the rights attached to each book. Tomeio's bundled providers focus on public-domain and openly available material.
